The owned graphics test fixture vendors unmodified MinHook v1.3.4 at commit
`c3fcafdc10146beb5919319d0683e44e3c30d537`, including Hacker Disassembler Engine.
Its copyright notices, redistribution terms, and disclaimers are retained in
[MinHook's license](prototype/graphics/vendor/minhook/LICENSE.txt) and source files.

records the upstream source and individual file hashes.

CrossOver, Wine, D3DMetal, GameToMac, Rockstar Launcher, Social Club, and GTA V
binaries are not included. Users supply their own installations. The two small
callback `.bin` files in `memory_only` are compiled from this repository's C
sources; they contain no extracted game, Wine, or graphics-runtime code.
